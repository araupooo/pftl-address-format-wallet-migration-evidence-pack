PFTL Address-Format and Wallet Migration — Results

Live testnet (network 2025, postfiatd 1.0.9), probed 2026-11-22 22:23 UTC. Validated ledger seq 8,281,677. Pinned postfiatd commit 0085a1ff885d6a446369cf73a4430f75af22be55.

Verdict: SOUND BUT FRAGILE AT THE EDGES — the address formats are correct and interoperable across three independent implementations, and the node rejects malformed input; every material risk sits at a boundary: curve choice, network identity, and client-side-only X-addresses. The same seed gives two different accounts depending on the curve (Ed25519 vs secp256k1), classic addresses carry no network identity (the same address is valid on testnet and mainnet), and X-addresses — the only format that distinguishes networks and carries a destination tag — do not exist in the node at all: the node rejects them with actMalformed.
